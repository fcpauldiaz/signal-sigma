Signal Sigma privacy

Last updated August 13, 2026.

Signal Sigma is a personal trading desk for the operator’s own Signal Sigma and Tradier accounts. It is not a public brokerage and does not open accounts for other people.

What the app stores on the device

The unlock token and paper/live mode preference are stored in the iOS Keychain and app storage so you do not have to sign in on every launch. You can clear them with Lock.

What the app sends

After unlock, the app talks only to the operator’s self-hosted desk at signal-sigma.coolify.chapilabs.com. That server already holds Signal Sigma and Tradier credentials. The iOS app does not send data to Expo, analytics vendors, advertisers, or other third parties.

What we do not collect

No advertising ID, no crash analytics, no location, no contacts, and no tracking across other companies’ apps.
